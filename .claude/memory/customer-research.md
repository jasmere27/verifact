## Regulatory / positioning (affects selling)
- FTC v. DoNotPay (final order 2025): no "performs like a lawyer" claims without evidence; $193k relief (FACT, ftc.gov).
- UPL is state-specific; e.g. Texas exempts software that clearly says it's not a substitute for an attorney (FACT, Tex. Gov't Code §81.101); application to conversational AI untested. Keep LegalFact professional-facing.
- Routing consumers to lawyers → ABA Model Rule 7.2 comment on lead generators; fee arrangements may be fee-sharing (FACT, americanbar.org). Needs legal review before any routing.
